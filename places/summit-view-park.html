Quick Tips

  • Most falls and viewpoints are best visited in the morning, before the crowds and the heat.
  • Bring cash, since entrance fees at most spots are small and do not take credit/debit cards.
  • Always check the weather first before heading to cliffside or coastal spots.

Nature & Cliffside View · Viga, Catanduanes

Summit View Park

Summit View Park is a scenic mountain viewpoint in Viga, Catanduanes, known for its beautiful sunrise views, where on good days, the surrounding hills gets blanketed in a dreamy sea of clouds. Located along the Viga highway, the park offers a peaceful view of the surrounding landscape and is a great place for taking photos and enjoying the scenery. The Summit View Park is a destination that shows the beauty of Catanduanes from a different perspective. It is a great place to take a break, breathe in the fresh air, and enjoy the view.
