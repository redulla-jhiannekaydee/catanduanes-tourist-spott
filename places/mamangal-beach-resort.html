Mamangal Beach


Maribina Falls is one of the most famous and accessible waterfalls in Bato, Catanduanes, with its refreshingly cold water and scenic surroundings that can wash your worries away. Aside from enjoying the beautiful scenery, you can also create more memories with your loved ones, family, or friends while spending time here. Maribina Falls is a great place to swim, especially during the summer season, because of its cold and refreshing water. You can also sit on the rocks near the water and simply savor the fresh air and the natural beauty of Maribina Falls. It is a perfect place to relax, have fun, and enjoy a refreshing escape with the people you love.
