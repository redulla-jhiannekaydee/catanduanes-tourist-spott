Binurong Point


Binurong Point is an astonishing destination located in Baras, Catanduanes, known for its rolling green hills, dramatic cliffs, and panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean. Visitors can take a short trek to reach the viewpoint, where they can enjoy the beautiful landscape, take photos, have a picnic, or simply appreciate the peaceful surroundings. When you reach the viewpoint, the sound of crashing waves and open sea breeze make it feel worlds away from the noise of the city. Its unique landscape represents the natural beauty that makes the province special. I picked this place because its spectacular view offers a memorable experience to explore the beauty of Catanduanes.
