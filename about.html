Jhianne Kaydee Redulla

Welcome to Catanduanes Tourist Spots site! This is your new gateway to discover the nature and the happiest corner of Catanduanes, the Happy Island.

Hello, my name is Kaydee, the owner behind this site. I was born in Catanduanes, and even though I haven’t spent my whole life there, the island province has always been part of who I am. I created this guide because I believe that Catanduanes deserves to be witnessed for its beauty. The sound of the waves, the beauty of the mountains, the warmth of the people, and the spots that you cannot take your eyes off when you visit – these are what makes Catanduanes special.

I haven’t been to most of the spots yet, but what I can tell you is this: once you step your foot to Catanduanes, it’s not just about the places; it’s all about the island itself. My recommendations came from what I searched or witnessed, and from the stories of the people and my relatives in the province. Overall, I hope you get to explore the beauty of Catanduanes itself!
